What to explain and prepare

A useful first call doesn't require technical language. Note when the problem began, what you observed, and whether it has changed. If more than one fixture or area is involved, mention that as well. Share any steps you've already taken, including shutting off a valve, but don't take apart equipment or handle electrical components to investigate.

Before a plumber arrives, consider preparing the work area:

  • Clear a path to the affected sink, toilet, appliance, or pipe area.
  • Keep children and pets away from standing water or tools.
  • Make a note of questions about the work and any disruption at home.
  • If water is running where it shouldn't, use a shutoff only if you can reach and operate it safely.
