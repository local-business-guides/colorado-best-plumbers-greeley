What to explain before plumbing work

A phone call is more useful when you have a few basic details at hand. Try to note:

  • Which room or fixture is affected
  • Whether the issue is constant or comes and goes
  • When you first noticed it
  • Any visible water, unusual sound, or change in how the fixture works
  • Whether you’ve already tried a simple fix

This gives the plumber a starting point without asking you to guess at the cause. Before work begins, make sure you understand the proposed work and ask about anything that is unclear. Avoid taking apart pipes or fixtures if you’re not comfortable doing so. A temporary fix can sometimes hide the full problem or make it harder to explain what happened.
